### Multi-child Discount

The first child is always full price. Every additional child receives a \$250 discount on tuition. For example, if you
have three children enrolled, the first child pays \$5,000, the second child pays \$4,750, and the third child pays
\$4,750.

### Scholarships

We are proud to accept the Arete Scholarship, which is a need-based scholarship that helps families afford Christian
education. Families must apply for the Arete Scholarship through the Arete Scholarship Foundation. For more information,
please visit [Arete Scholarship Foundation](https://aretescholarship.org/).
