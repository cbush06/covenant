The admissions process is simple, personal, and designed to help you and your family determine if CCA is the right fit
for your child.

{{< figure src="images/admissions/process.png" alt="CCA Admissions Process" title="CCA Admissions Process" >}}

### Step 1: Express Interest
To start the process, please fill out the [online application](./apply/). We will contact you to schedule a tour and
answer any questions you may have about CCA.

You are also welcome to call the school at <a href="tel:+12293521472">(229)352-1472</a> to speak with our admissions
team directly.

### Step 2: Tour
Come visit us! We would love to show you around our campus and introduce you to our teachers and staff. 
During your tour, you will have the opportunity to see our classrooms, meet our students, and learn more about 
our curriculum and extracurricular activities.

Our address and contact information can be found on our [contact page](../contact/).

### Step 3: Enroll
Once you have decided to enroll your child at CCA, please complete the enrollment paperwork and submit it along with
required documents. Our admissions team will review your application and contact you to schedule a diagnostic test for
your child.

### Step 4: Diagnostic Test
Your child will take the A.C.E. School of Tomorrow Diagnostic Test. This is a placement assessment designed to determine
a student’s skill and concept mastery before beginning the A.C.E. curriculum.  The tests identify academic gaps and
prescribe specific gap PACEs to strengthen weaknesses, ensuring students are placed at their exact performance level
rather than by age or grade.

### Step 5: Plan Course of Study
We will use the diagnostic test results to create a personalized course of study for your child. Among other things,
this plan will specify where in the P.A.C.E. curriculum your child will begin.

### Step 6: Begin Classes
Finally, your child can begin a rewarding, biblical, and fulfilling educational experience at Covenant Christian 
Academy!
