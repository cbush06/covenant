Christian Covenant Academy started as a thought. Like a seed, God grew the thought into action. Every step we took in
obedience to God was met with provision. Christian Covenant Church readily opened their doors and hearts to allow us to
begin a school in their facility. At the same time, Light House Christian School in Valdosta took us under their wings
and mentored us through hands-on education and training seminars.

Sadly, Christian Covenant Church had to close their doors, but God opened another door for us to continue our journey.
We are now located in Life Church at 588 Fussell Road, Leesburg, GA.
