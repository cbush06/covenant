## Mission Statement

> The Mission of Christian Covenant Academy
> is to help parents train students to serve God
> by providing an excellent academic education
> while developing areas of Christian leadership,
> respect, self-discipline, obedience and patriotism.

## Vision Statement

> To passionately love God Wholeheartedly.
> 
> To provide a solid and consistent Bible based education.
> 
> To teach, train, equip, and activate the young body of Christ
> into their gifting and calling.
> 
> To create a Holy Spirit filled educational environment that
> encourages a boldness and willingness to serve the body
> of Christ, our community, and the nation.
