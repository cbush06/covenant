### About Me

Lisa has been a missionary to several foreign countries with International Children's Ministry Institute and other
organizations. She has organized and led an inner city Sidewalk Sunday School for 15 years with the help of
countless other volunteers. She taught Sunday School many of those years and helped facilitate a homework club for inner
city children. She was certified by the Child Development Association and is currently obtaining an Associates Degree in
Early Childhood Education and Development at Albany Technical College. Lisa has taught at a previous Christian school
and is grateful for that opportunity and encouragement. Since 2018, she has been the School Administrator and Teacher of
the middle and high school students here at CCA.
